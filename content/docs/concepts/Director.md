# Director

In the movie pattern, the director is like the director of a movie, ensuring everything works together.
While actors play their roles, the director ensures that the whole performance is cohesive and smooth.

## The Role of the Director

The Director coordinates the entire application.
This role involves envisioning, instructing, and unifying various parts to create a seamless performance.

## Responsibilities of the Director

To achieve these tasks, the Director uses:

- **Composition**: Ensuring each scenario is complete with all necessary components.
- **Instruction**: Guiding actors to perform their roles effectively.

### Composing the Scene

Like a film director, the Director ensures each scenario is complete.
They add missing components and refine details.

### Instructing the Actors

The director provides guidance to each role.
They offer additional skills, refine existing ones, and help roles interact effectively within the scene.

## Summary

The director in the movie pattern is the leader who brings everything together.
They ensure every scenario is well-composed, every actor is well-instructed, and the production runs smoothly.
The director's role is crucial for a cohesive and effective application, much like a well-conducted orchestra.
